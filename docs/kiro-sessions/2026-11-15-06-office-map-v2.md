**How each fix landed**
- **Spawns:** (1,4), (9,4), (2,11) and (9,12), at least 6.7 tiles from the player's desk. The build checks this and fails if a spawn is closer. The player's desk is now on the right.
- **Skeleton:** seated in profile at the right end of a desk, with a headset, bent at the hips and thighs under the desk. Its head stays clear of the monitor.
- **Doormat:** it sits on the floor inside the entrance. I narrowed it so it no longer overlaps the EXIT pole sign.
- **Hide spots:** 8 spots (desk with a cloth skirt, locker, supply closet, sofa, meeting table, plant divider, boxes, wardrobe). The script reports that the farthest walkable tile is 4.12 tiles from a spot and fails above 5.
- **Behind the sofa:** the sofa stands free on a plum rug with a clear lane behind it. I added a floor shadow band there and extended the annotation box over the gap.
- **Annotated preview:** the numbers are 6x scale (they were 3x). The legend sits in a strip below the room.
- **Night preview:** the ghost has an ice-blue light pool and the crawler an orange one.

**What I'm not happy with**
- The side walls have a thin dark border and are cream, not as dark and boxy as the reference.
- The null-ghost placed at spawn (9,4) overlaps the monitor of the skeleton's neighbouring desk. It reads fine for a floating ghost.
- Two details are less clear than the rest. The skeleton is only about 30px tall in the 1x sprite, so its profile is easy to miss. The behind-sofa gap reads as a dark band and a visible lane, and I didn't add a clearer cue.
- The supply closet's hide tile (15,3) is right beside the server-door clear zone. Golem slots are (16,4) and (18,4).
- I haven't checked the new `office.json` against `OfficeScene.ts`. The scene only mentions the office in a comment, so I don't know what it expects.

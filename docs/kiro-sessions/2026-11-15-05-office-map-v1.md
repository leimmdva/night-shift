## Prompt given to Kiro

**Run 1: first prompt**

````markdown
Create a 2.5D pixel-art preview of the Night Shift office map. For now the office is ONE room. Use the same toolkit and style as the monster sprites (scripts/sprites/draw/, 2.5D top-down 3/4 camera, light from the top-left, dark outlines in each material's darkest shade, 4-step ramps, no anti-aliasing). Static images only.

THE ROOM
- Grid: 20 x 14 tiles, 32px per tile (640 x 448 px at 1x). Camera: top-down 3/4, so the back (top) wall shows its front face about 2 tiles tall; the side walls show as thin wall tops; the floor is seen from above.
- A small startup office at night (company: Mortem Labs, fictional). Carpet tiles or worn office floor, back wall with two tall night windows (dark blue sky, a moon, city lights far away), a wall clock showing 3:00, a whiteboard with scribbled diagrams and the text "SHIP IT FRIDAY" crossed out.
- Furniture: 3–4 rows/clusters of desks with chairs, monitors and keyboards; one desk is the PLAYER'S DESK with an open laptop glowing amber (#E8A33D); a kitchenette corner (coffee machine, fridge, water cooler); a printer; plants; a sofa; filing cabinets.
- A heavy SERVER ROOM DOOR in the back wall, top-right, with an amber "SERVER" sign and a small window with green/amber LEDs. Leave the floor in front of it empty (two Deadlock golems will stand there later).
- An entrance door bottom-left.

HIDING SPOTS (at least 6, each must look like something you can hide in or behind, and be spread around the room so every area has one within ~5 tiles):
1. Under a desk with a long cloth/cover hanging down (desk with a skirt)
2. A tall metal locker
3. A supply closet with a half-open door
4. Behind the sofa
5. Under the big meeting table (cloth to the floor)
6. Behind a large potted plant / room divider
(you may add a cardboard-box stack behind the printer as a 7th)

HALLOWEEN DECORATIONS (it must be obvious at first glance that it is Halloween):
- Orange and purple string lights along the back wall and windows (bright pixel bulbs)
- Carved jack-o'-lanterns on several desks and by the entrance, glowing from inside
- Cobwebs in the top corners and on the server door, a hanging spider
- Paper bats taped to the windows
- A "HAPPY HALLOWEEN" paper garland/banner on the back wall
- A candy bowl on the kitchenette counter, candy wrappers on the floor
- A plastic skeleton sitting in one office chair wearing a headset
- A witch hat on a monitor, fake gravestone foam prop near the sofa
Keep it fun and spooky, not gory.

OUTPUTS
- Build the room from reusable pieces so the game can use them later: tiles (floor, wall front, wall top, window, door) and props (each desk type, chairs, locker, closet, sofa, plants, decorations) as separate PNGs in public/sprites/office/, sources in scripts/sprites/ like the monsters.
- A layout file src/game/maps/office.json describing the grid, the position of every prop, and a list "hideSpots" (id, tile x/y, type), "playerDesk", "serverDoor", "entrance" and "monsterSpawns" (3–4 sensible spawn tiles).
- docs/art/office-preview.png: the full composed room at 2x scale (1280 x 896), lights ON (Act 1): ceiling lights on, readable, warm.
- docs/art/office-preview-night.png: the same room at 2x, lights OFF (Act 2): very dark blue overlay, only the windows/moonlight, jack-o'-lantern glows, string lights, monitor/laptop glow, server LEDs and EXIT sign stay lit (draw these as pixel light pools, not blurry gradients). Place the null-ghost sprite and the infinite-crawler sprite in the room for scale.
- docs/art/office-preview-annotated.png: the lit version with each hide spot outlined in green (#A9F07E) and numbered, player desk in amber, monster spawns as red (#C8402F) X marks, server door in red. Add a small legend.
- npm script "build-office" (or extend build-sprites) that regenerates everything.

CHECK BEFORE YOU COMMIT
- Open all three previews and check: the room reads as one office from a 3/4 top-down view, every hide spot is recognisable, there is a clear walkable path between all areas (at least 1 tile wide, ideally 2), Halloween is obvious, nothing floats or clips through walls, and objects sit on the floor with contact shadows. Fix and re-run until it looks good.
- Run the build scripts, npx vitest run and npm run build; all must pass.
- Follow the dev-log steering rule (append a Timeline entry to docs/KIRO_LOG.md and fill in the previous entry's commit hash 53b32f3).
- Commit as "feat: 2.5D office map preview with hiding spots and Halloween decor". Do not push.
- Print the list of files and anything you are not happy with.
````
